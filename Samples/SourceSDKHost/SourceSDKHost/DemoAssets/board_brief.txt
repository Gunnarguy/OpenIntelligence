Board Briefing

OpenIntelligence is building a private Apple-native document intelligence engine for sensitive document workflows.

Immediate wedge:
- technical manuals
- board packets
- diligence notes
- internal research

Why it matters:
Teams want AI help with private documents without casually routing them through generic hosted workflows.

Short-term product goal:
Ship a source-distributed SDK that can ingest documents, create libraries, answer grounded questions, and return citations inside a separate iOS host app.
