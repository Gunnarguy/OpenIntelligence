OpenIntelligence GTM Memo
Early commercial plan

Core thesis
OpenIntelligence should enter the market through founder-led design-partner deals where private document trust is more valuable than broad feature breadth.

Ideal first customer profile
- Apple-native team
- Sensitive internal documents
- Frequent high-stakes meetings
- Clear pain around searching through board materials, diligence packets, research notes, or compliance documentation
- Willingness to run a scoped evaluation before asking for full productization

Commercial wedge
- "Trusted answers from your private documents, with citations."
- The goal is not replacing human judgment.
- The goal is compressing the time between "where is that buried?" and "here is the answer with evidence."

Why now
- Teams are actively exploring AI, but trust in uncited answers remains low.
- Privacy and data-boundary concerns create space for an Apple-native story.
- Executives and operators are overwhelmed by document volume during a period of higher scrutiny and faster decision cycles.

Pilot offer
- 4-week technical evaluation followed by a 6-week design-partner pilot.
- Pricing target: 25 thousand dollars for a light evaluation, up to 40 thousand dollars for a higher-touch pilot.
- Success metrics: faster prep for recurring meetings, less manual document search, and higher confidence in answers because the evidence is visible.

Sample ROI framing
- If one leadership team saves 90 minutes of preparation before a weekly high-stakes meeting, the product is paying for real executive time almost immediately.
- If investment or diligence teams can cut packet search time materially, the product becomes valuable before any broader workflow automation exists.

Positioning guidance
- Lead with private docs and trust.
- Show citations early.
- Avoid overselling general AI breadth.
- Be honest that the current SDK path is evaluation-ready and founder-guided.

What to say in the room
- "We are not asking you to trust a black box."
- "We are showing you a private document engine that answers and points to the source."
- "If the evidence is weak, the system should make that clear instead of bluffing."

Next-step goal after the demo
- Secure a paid design-partner conversation.
- Offer a scoped technical evaluation against the buyer's own document set.
- Keep the follow-up anchored on one concrete workflow with economic value, not a generic innovation budget ask.
