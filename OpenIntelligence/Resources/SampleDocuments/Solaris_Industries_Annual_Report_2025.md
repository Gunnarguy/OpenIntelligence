# Solaris Industries Annual Report 2025

## Fiscal Year Ending December 31, 2025

---

## Letter from the CEO

Dear Shareholders,

I am pleased to report that fiscal year 2025 represents a transformational period for Solaris Industries. We achieved **$4.27 billion in revenue**, a 23% increase from the prior year, while maintaining strong profitability with a net income of **$487 million**.

Our strategic focus on renewable energy solutions has positioned Solaris at the forefront of the global energy transition. The successful launch of the Helios X7 solar panel series and our expansion into battery storage with the PowerVault product line exceeded all internal projections.

Key achievements this year include:

- Opened our third manufacturing facility in Austin, Texas
- Reached 2 million residential installations globally
- Launched the Solaris Energy app with 1.2 million active users
- Achieved carbon-neutral operations across all facilities
- Named to Fortune's "Most Admired Companies" list

Looking ahead to 2026, we are investing heavily in next-generation technology. Our R&D budget of $340 million represents 8% of revenue—the highest in our company's history. We expect to launch the Helios X8 series in Q3 2026 with 28% efficiency, setting a new industry benchmark.

Thank you for your continued trust in Solaris Industries.

Sincerely,

**Dr. Elena Rodriguez**
Chief Executive Officer
Solaris Industries

## Business Segment Review

The Residential Solar division generated **$2.14 billion** in revenue, representing 50.1% of total company revenue. We installed **247,000 new residential systems** across 18 countries.

**Key Products:**

| Product            | Efficiency | Warranty | Price/Watt |
| ------------------ | ---------- | -------- | ---------- |
| Helios X7 Premium  | 24.2%      | 30 years | $2.85      |
| Helios X7 Standard | 22.1%      | 25 years | $2.45      |
| Helios X5 (legacy) | 20.3%      | 25 years | $2.15      |

**Regional Performance:**

| Region        | Installations | Revenue | YoY Growth |

## Research & Development

R&D investment totaled **$340 million** in FY 2025, representing 8.0% of revenue.

### Key Initiatives

**Next-Generation Solar Cells:**

- Helios X8 development: 28% efficiency target (current record: 26.4%)
- Perovskite tandem cell research: 32% efficiency achieved in lab conditions
- Partnership with Stanford University on quantum dot technology

**Battery Technology:**

- Solid-state battery prototype: 40% higher energy density than current lithium-ion
- Faster charging: 80% charge in 45 minutes (vs. current 90 minutes)
- Extended lifespan: 8,000 cycle target (vs. current 6,000 cycles)

**Software & AI:**

- Predictive maintenance algorithms: 34% reduction in unplanned service calls
- Energy optimization AI: Average 12% improvement in system output
- Grid integration: Vehicle-to-home (V2H) compatibility in development

### Strategic Priorities

1. **Launch Helios X8:** Target Q3 2026 with 28% efficiency
2. **Battery Capacity Expansion:** Add 1.5 GWh capacity at Austin facility
3. **International Expansion:** Enter Australian and Brazilian markets
4. **Vertical Integration:** Acquire inverter or mounting system manufacturer

### Risk Factors

- Potential expiration of U.S. Investment Tax Credit
- Competition from Chinese manufacturers with lower costs
- Supply chain disruptions affecting silicon or lithium
- Interest rate impact on residential financing
- Regulatory changes in key markets
