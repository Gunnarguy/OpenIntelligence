## Chapter 1: Vehicle Specifications

### 1.1 Powertrain

The Aurora EV-7 features a dual-motor all-wheel-drive system producing **487 horsepower** and **612 lb-ft of torque**. The front motor generates 201 hp while the rear motor produces 286 hp.

| Specification      | Value                            |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Total System Power | 487 hp (363 kW)                  |
| Total Torque       | 612 lb-ft (830 Nm)               |
| 0-60 mph           | 3.8 seconds                      |
| Top Speed          | 155 mph (electronically limited) |
| Curb Weight        | 4,847 lbs (2,199 kg)             |

### 1.2 Battery System

The EV-7 is equipped with a **98.4 kWh lithium-ion battery pack** manufactured by CellTech Industries. The battery consists of 7,104 cylindrical cells arranged in 16 modules.

**EPA Estimated Range:** 347 miles (combined cycle)

| Charging Method  | Time to Full        | Rate        |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ----------- |
| Level 1 (120V)   | 72 hours            | 1.4 kW      |
| Level 2 (240V)   | 9.5 hours           | 11.5 kW     |
| DC Fast Charging | 28 minutes (10-80%) | 250 kW peak |

**Important:** The battery thermal management system maintains optimal temperature between 68°F and 86°F (20°C-30°C). Charging in extreme temperatures may be automatically limited to protect battery longevity.

### 1.3 Dimensions

- **Length:** 196.4 inches (4,989 mm)
- **Width:** 77.2 inches (1,961 mm)
- **Height:** 56.8 inches (1,443 mm)
- **Wheelbase:** 116.5 inches (2,959 mm)
- **Ground Clearance:** 5.1 inches (130 mm)
- **Cargo Volume:** 15.3 cubic feet (rear) + 2.8 cubic feet (front trunk)

## Chapter 2: Fluids and Maintenance

### 2.1 Fluid Specifications

Unlike internal combustion vehicles, the EV-7 requires minimal fluid maintenance. However, certain systems still require periodic service.

#### Brake Fluid

- **Type:** DOT 4 synthetic brake fluid
- **Capacity:** 1.2 liters
- **Replacement Interval:** Every 2 years or 25,000 miles
- **Recommended Brand:** Aurora Genuine Brake Fluid (Part #AUR-BF-DOT4)

#### Coolant (Battery and Motor)

- **Type:** Aurora E-Coolant G48 (ethylene glycol-based, silicate-free)
- **Capacity:** 12.7 liters (total system)
- **Replacement Interval:** Every 4 years or 50,000 miles
- **Color:** Blue (do NOT mix with other colors)

**⚠️ WARNING:** Using incorrect coolant may void your warranty and cause severe damage to the battery thermal management system. Only use Aurora E-Coolant G48 or equivalent meeting specification AUR-TMS-2024.

#### Windshield Washer Fluid

- **Capacity:** 4.5 liters
- **Type:** Any commercial washer fluid rated to -20°F (-29°C)

#### Reduction Gear Oil

- **Type:** Aurora Synthetic Gear Oil 75W-90
- **Front Motor:** 0.8 liters
- **Rear Motor:** 1.1 liters
- **Replacement Interval:** Every 100,000 miles or 8 years (whichever comes first)

### 2.2 Tire Specifications

The EV-7 comes equipped with **Michelin Pilot Sport EV** tires specifically designed for electric vehicles.

| Position | Size      | Pressure (Cold)  |
| -------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| Front    | 245/40R20 | 42 PSI (2.9 bar) |
| Rear     | 275/35R20 | 44 PSI (3.0 bar) |

**Rotation Schedule:** Every 7,500 miles in a front-to-rear pattern only (do not cross-rotate due to staggered sizes).

### 2.3 Maintenance Schedule

| Interval            | Service Required                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Every 7,500 miles   | Tire rotation, cabin air filter inspection       |

## Chapter 3: Safety Systems

### 3.1 Aurora Shield (ADAS)

The EV-7 includes Aurora Shield, a comprehensive advanced driver assistance system with the following features:

- **Adaptive Cruise Control:** Maintains set speed and following distance (0.8 to 3.0 seconds adjustable)
- **Lane Centering Assist:** Active steering to maintain lane position
- **Automatic Emergency Braking:** Activates at speeds up to 90 mph
- **Blind Spot Monitoring:** Alerts via door mirror indicators
- **Cross-Traffic Alert:** Rear and front detection during low-speed maneuvers

**System Limitations:** Aurora Shield cameras require cleaning in rain, snow, or dusty conditions. The system may not detect:

- Motorcycles approaching from behind at high speed differential (>30 mph)
- Pedestrians wearing dark clothing at night
- Objects less than 18 inches in height

### 3.2 Battery Safety

The EV-7 battery pack includes multiple safety systems:

1. **Thermal Runaway Prevention:** 47 individual temperature sensors monitor cell temperatures
2. **Overcharge Protection:** Charging automatically stops at 100% state of charge
3. **Crash Disconnect:** High-voltage system disconnects within 50 milliseconds of airbag deployment
4. **Venting System:** Controlled gas venting in case of cell failure (vents toward ground)

**In case of battery fire:** Do NOT attempt to extinguish with water. Evacuate vehicle and call emergency services. Inform responders this is an electric vehicle with a 98.4 kWh battery.

### 3.3 Airbag System

The EV-7 contains **10 airbags**:

- 2 front (driver and passenger)
- 2 side torso (front seats)
- 2 side curtain (full-length)
- 2 knee (driver and passenger)
- 1 center (between front seats)
- 1 rear center (between rear outboard passengers)

**Airbag deployment threshold:** 14 mph (22.5 km/h) frontal impact equivalent

### 4.2 Battery Capacity Guarantee

Aurora guarantees that the EV-7 battery will retain at least **70% of its original capacity** for 8 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first. If capacity falls below 70% during this period, Aurora will:

1. First attempt recalibration and cell balancing
2. If unsuccessful, replace degraded modules
3. If more than 4 modules require replacement, replace entire pack

**Exclusions:** Battery degradation caused by:

- Consistent charging to 100% (recommended daily limit: 80%)
- Repeated DC fast charging (more than 80% of total charging sessions)
- Exposure to temperatures above 140°F (60°C)
- Unauthorized modifications

### 4.3 What Is NOT Covered

The following items are specifically excluded from warranty coverage:

- Normal wear items (brake pads, wiper blades, tires, cabin filters)
- Damage from accidents, misuse, or neglect
- Modifications not approved by Aurora
- Damage from using non-approved fluids (see Section 2.1)
- Cosmetic damage (paint chips, interior wear)
- Damage from natural disasters
- Loss of vehicle use during warranty repairs

## Chapter 5: Troubleshooting

### 5.1 Warning Lights

| Warning           | Meaning                          | Action Required                                                 |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 Red Battery    | Critical battery fault           | Stop safely, do not drive. Call Aurora Roadside: 1-800-AUR-HELP |
| 🟡 Yellow Battery | Battery temperature warning      | Reduce speed, allow cooling. Avoid fast charging.               |
| 🔴 Red Brake      | Brake system failure             | Stop immediately. Do not drive.                                 |
| 🟡 Yellow Brake   | Brake fluid low or wear detected | Schedule service within 500 miles                               |
| 🟡 Tire Pressure  | Tire pressure deviation >5 PSI   | Check and adjust tire pressures                                 |
| 🔴 Airbag         | Airbag system malfunction        | Service immediately. Safety system compromised.                 |

### 5.2 Common Issues

**Issue:** Vehicle will not charge
**Possible Causes:**

1. Charging port latch not fully engaged (push connector firmly until click)
2. Charging cable fault (try different cable)
3. Ground fault detected (check outlet grounding)
4. Battery temperature outside charging range (wait for temperature normalization)
5. 12V battery depleted (charge or replace 12V battery)

**Issue:** Reduced range in cold weather
**Expected Behavior:** Range reduction of 20-30% at temperatures below 32°F (0°C) is normal. The battery requires energy for thermal management, and cabin heating increases consumption.

**Issue:** Regenerative braking feels weak
**Possible Causes:**

1. Battery at high state of charge (>90%) - normal behavior
2. Battery temperature too cold - will increase as battery warms
3. Regen setting set to Low - check in Settings > Driving > Regen Level

### 5.3 Error Codes

If the vehicle displays a diagnostic code, record it and contact your dealer:

| Code                | Description        | Severity |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | -------- |
| E-001 through E-099 | Battery management | Critical |
| E-100 through E-199 | Motor/inverter     | High     |
| E-200 through E-299 | Charging system    | Medium   |
| E-300 through E-399 | ADAS/sensors       | Medium   |
| E-400 through E-499 | HVAC/comfort       | Low      |
| E-500 through E-599 | Infotainment       | Low      |
