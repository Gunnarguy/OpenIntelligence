OpenIntelligence turns your documents into a personal knowledge base you can actually talk to.

Drop in PDFs, notes, code, or research papers. Ask questions in plain language. Get answers that cite exactly where the information came from — with page numbers and passages you can verify.

This isn't a chatbot that makes things up. It's a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that grounds every answer in your actual documents.

▸ WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Most AI apps send your data to the cloud. OpenIntelligence processes everything on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence — the same technology that powers system-wide AI features in iOS 26.

Your documents stay on your device. When Apple's on-device model needs more power, it seamlessly extends to Private Cloud Compute — Apple Silicon servers with cryptographic guarantees of zero data retention. No OpenAI. No third-party servers. No fine print.

▸ THE PIPELINE

Behind a simple chat interface is a production-grade retrieval system:

• Semantic chunking preserves document structure and meaning
• CoreML embeddings convert text into 384-dimensional vectors
• Hybrid search combines keyword matching (BM25) with semantic similarity
• Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges ranking signals for better results
• Cross-encoder reranking prioritizes the most relevant passages
• Maximal Marginal Relevance removes redundancy from context

This is the same architecture used by enterprise knowledge systems — running entirely on your phone.

▸ ORGANIZE BY PROJECT

Create separate knowledge containers for different projects. Your work research stays isolated from personal notes. Each container maintains its own vector index for fast, focused retrieval.

▸ SUPPORTED FORMATS

• Documents: PDF, TXT, Markdown, RTF
• Images: PNG, JPEG, HEIC (Vision OCR extracts text automatically)
• Code: Swift, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Go, Rust, and 20+ languages
• Data: JSON, YAML, CSV

• No cloud sync unless you explicitly enable Private Cloud Compute
