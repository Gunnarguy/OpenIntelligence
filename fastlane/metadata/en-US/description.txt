Ask your own documents questions on iPhone and review source-backed answers with citations.

OpenIntelligence imports your files into private libraries, searches them locally, and answers from the material you provide. The product focus is not generic chat. It is document-grounded question answering with source review, clearer trust signals, and refusal when support is weak.

WHY PEOPLE USE IT
• Ask questions over manuals, notes, research papers, policies, support docs, and internal reference material
• Get answers with citations and jump back to the source passages
• Review which claims were supported, which were dropped, and what evidence was missing
• Keep search, indexing, and most answering behavior on-device by default

TRUST FEATURES
• Source-backed answers with citations
• Claim-level verification before final answer rendering
• Grounded refusal when critical claims cannot be supported
• Source review with document, page, and excerpt inspection
• Multiple quality modes depending on how deep you want the app to reason

PRIVACY
OpenIntelligence is designed around Apple platforms and local execution. Search, storage, indexing, and document preparation stay on your device. Apple-managed cloud processing may be used only through Apple system features and your device settings when the platform makes that available.

SUPPORTED CONTENT
Documents: PDF, TXT, MD, RTF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, JSON, XML, YAML, CSV
Code and data: Swift, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, C, C++, Go, Rust, Ruby, PHP, HTML, CSS, SQL, Shell, and more
Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF
Audio and video: common Apple-supported formats with local extraction and transcription where available

QUALITY MODES
• Standard for fast document answers
• Deep Think for harder multi-step questions
• Maximum for heavier synthesis when you want the app to spend more effort

SUBSCRIPTIONS
• Free tier with limited document capacity
• Pro monthly and annual plans for larger libraries
• Lifetime option for one-time unlock access

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.
