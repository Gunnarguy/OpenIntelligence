Turn your documents into an offline neural knowledge base — entirely on your iPhone.

OpenIntelligence is an on-device RAG engine that imports your documents, builds a local search index, and answers questions using Apple Intelligence. No cloud. No accounts. No data leaves your device.

RETRIEVAL (29-STEP PIPELINE)
• Hybrid Search — BM25 keywords fused with 384-dim neural embeddings via RRF
• HyDE — Synthetic answer generation for better semantic matching
• Cross-Encoder Reranking — Neural model rescores candidates for precision
• MMR Diversification — Filters redundant chunks for maximum coverage
• Parent Document Retrieval — Expands matches to surrounding paragraph context
• Graph Context Packing — Optimizes the 4,096-token budget via graph algorithms
• Lost-in-Middle Mitigation — Reorders evidence so the AI sees critical content first

DOCUMENT PROCESSING
• Semantic Chunking — Splits by logic and structure, not character counts
• Multi-Column OCR — Metal-accelerated Vision at 360 DPI for complex layouts
• Font Cipher Detection — Detects encoded text and forces full OCR
• Entity Extraction — Names, organizations, technical terms indexed per document

ANTI-HALLUCINATION (7 GATES)
Every answer passes gates A-G: retrieval confidence, evidence coverage, numeric sanity, contradiction detection, semantic grounding, quote faithfulness, and generation quality. Critical failures trigger abstention — the app refuses to answer rather than answer wrong.

When questions barely overlap with documents, Evidence-First Mode states what evidence exists instead of fabricating answers.

THREE QUALITY MODES
• Standard — Fast factual answers in seconds
• Deep Think — 4-8 reasoning sessions with iterative retrieval for legal, medical, or academic research
• Maximum — 8-50 sessions with exhaustive multi-chain synthesis

AI HUB TRANSFORMS
Extract Key Facts, Step-by-Step, Plain English, Gap Analysis, or Illustrations from any answer — grounded in source documents with markdown rendering.

AGENTIC TOOL USE
8 tools the AI invokes automatically: semantic search, document listing, summarization, pattern counting, exact text search, corpus statistics, related documents, and cross-document comparison.

PERFORMANCE & TELEMETRY

100% PRIVATE
