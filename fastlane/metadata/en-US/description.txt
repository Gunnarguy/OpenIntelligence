No login required. No account creation. OpenIntelligence works offline by default.

OpenIntelligence is a local-first document intelligence app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Import files into private libraries, ask natural-language questions, and inspect the exact passages behind each answer. Core parsing, OCR, indexing, retrieval, and library management do not require a developer-operated cloud.

WHAT YOU CAN DO:

• Ask grounded questions over PDFs, Office documents, notes, spreadsheets, code, images, and transcriptable audio or video.
• Inspect source cards and excerpts instead of treating answers like a black box.
• Use Standard for fast grounded answers, or choose Deep Think and Maximum for higher-effort reasoning.
• Search embedded PDF figures and structured table evidence when the source supports it.
• Turn answers into formats like Key Facts, Step-by-Step, Plain English, and Gap Analysis.
• Keep work separated in private libraries instead of one giant mixed corpus.

BEST SUPPORTED CONTENT:
Documents: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, Markdown, CSV, RTF, TXT, HTML, JSON, XML, YAML, and source code.
Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF.
Audio & Video (transcription): MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, MP4, MOV.

CURRENT LIMITS:
Some dense table-heavy PDFs, legacy Office files, Apple iWork files, and unusual structured exports may still need cleanup or conversion for the cleanest results. OpenIntelligence is strongest on modern PDFs, Office documents, notes, code, images, and transcriptable media.

PRIVACY:
No analytics. No tracking. Core document parsing, OCR, SQLite full-text indexing, vector indexing, and retrieval stay on your hardware for the app's main workflows. Generation uses Apple Foundation Models where available. No developer-operated cloud is required for core document Q&A.

SUBSCRIPTIONS:
• Free tier: 5 documents, 1 library, full grounded pipeline, all quality modes. Maximum mode enforces a daily free quota.
• Pro Monthly / Annual: 1,000 documents, 5 separate libraries.
• Lifetime unlock: Unlimited documents, 10 libraries, one-time purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID on confirmation. Subscriptions renew unless canceled. Manage in account settings.
