Ask your documents anything. Get answers with citations.

OpenIntelligence is a document question-answering app that runs entirely on your device. Import PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, or any text file. Ask questions in plain English. Get accurate, cited answers powered by on-device AI.

YOUR DOCUMENTS, YOUR DEVICE

Unlike cloud-based AI services, OpenIntelligence processes everything locally. Your files never leave your iPhone or iPad unless you explicitly enable Private Cloud Compute for complex queries. When PCC is used, Apple's zero-retention policy ensures your data is never stored or logged.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Import any document (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, MD, CSV, RTF, or images)
2. Ask a question about its contents
3. Receive an answer with exact citations to the source text
4. Tap any citation to verify the original passage

POWERED BY ON-DEVICE AI

• On-Device AI: Uses iOS Foundation Models running directly on your device's Neural Engine
• Private Cloud Compute: Optional fallback for complex queries—same Apple privacy guarantees
• Execution Badges: Every response shows exactly where it was processed (📱 On-Device or ☁️ PCC)

SMART RETRIEVAL TECHNOLOGY

OpenIntelligence uses advanced retrieval techniques to find the right information:

• Hybrid Search — Combines keyword matching with semantic understanding
• Neural Reranking — AI-powered relevance scoring for precise results
• Intelligent Chunking — Preserves document structure and context
• Multi-Column Layout Detection — Correctly reads complex PDFs
• 8 Agentic Tools — The AI can search, summarize, compare, and analyze across your library

KEY FEATURES

• Document Library: Organize files into containers with automatic tagging
• Quality Modes: Standard for quick answers, Deep Think for thorough research
• Siri Integration: Ask questions via voice or Shortcuts
• Real-Time Telemetry: See retrieval scores, generation speed, and confidence levels
• No Account Required: Works completely offline with no sign-up

SUPPORTED FORMATS

PDF (native text + OCR for scans), Microsoft Office (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX), Plain text (TXT, MD, CSV, RTF), Images (JPEG, PNG, HEIC with text extraction)

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

• No third-party analytics or tracking
• Your documents are stored only in your app sandbox
