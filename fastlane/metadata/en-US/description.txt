No login required. No account creation. The app works entirely offline.

OpenIntelligence is a 100% on-device autonomous AI agent with multi-step reasoning and Neural RAG for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. Import your documents, and the app indexes them into a locally-stored SQLite database. When queried, it executes a 29-step retrieval pipeline to answer natural language questions with exact cited sources. Every single query is processed natively using Apple Foundation Models. Zero network dependency for core functionality.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES:

• 29-Step Neural Pipeline: Full semantic chunking, hybrid search (BM25 + neural embeddings), cross-encoder reranking, MMR diversification, parent document retrieval, graph context packing, and lost-in-the-middle mitigation.
• 7 Anti-Hallucination Gates (A-G): Real-time checks for retrieval confidence, evidence coverage, numeric sanity, contradiction detection, semantic grounding, quote faithfulness, and generation quality. Tighter evidence verification prevents bluffs when support is weak.
• Grounded-QA Routing: Strict dual-pipeline evaluation paths. Fact-heavy questions are rigidly grounded; deep synthesis questions scale flexibly.
• Evidence-First Mode: When query-document overlap is low, the engine states available evidence directly instead of generating unsupported answers.
• 3 Quality Modes: Standard (single-pass fast responses), Deep Think (multi-session agentic reasoning), and Maximum (unlimited reasoning until 98% verified confidence).
• 8 Agentic Tools: Search, compare, summarize, timeline, extract, define, calculate, and analyze across your library.
• Adaptive Follow-Ups: Dynamic suggestions generated based on context flow.
• Active Library Starter Questions: Automatic contextual prompts built from a sample of active documents.
• AI Hub Transforms: Convert answers fluidly into Key Facts, Step-by-Steps, Plain English, Gap Analysis, and Illustrations.
• Advanced OCR Engine: Multi-column 360 DPI optical character recognition with font cipher detection.
• Motherboard HUD: A real-time Apple Silicon telemetry overlay to watch your device work.
• Dynamic Metal Shaders: 3-tier GPU optimizations that automatically adapt to your specific chip family (A15 through M4).
• Rich Markdown: Beautiful inline rendering for code blocks, tables, lists, quotes, math equations, and complex formats.

RESPONSE INSPECTION:
Zero black-box hallucinations. Every generated response includes deterministic source cards with explicit filenames and scrollable excerpts. You see exactly which passages the on-device Neural RAG engine used to synthesize its answer, providing complete confidence and transparency.

Zero third-party models. Zero analytics. All document parsing, embedding generation, OCR, and SQLite vector indexing stay strictly bound to your local hardware. Your data never leaves your absolute sovereignty.
