Turn your documents into an offline neural knowledge base — entirely on your iPhone.

OpenIntelligence is an on-device RAG engine (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) that imports your documents, builds a local search index, and answers questions using Apple Intelligence. No cloud. No accounts. No data leaves your device.

102 SERVICES. 29-STEP PIPELINE. ZERO CLOUD DEPENDENCY.

This is not keyword search. OpenIntelligence runs a full retrieval pipeline — the same architecture used by enterprise research systems — compressed into your pocket:

RETRIEVAL
• Hybrid Search — BM25 keyword matching fused with 384-dimensional neural embeddings via Reciprocal Rank Fusion
• HyDE — Hypothetical Document Embeddings generate synthetic answers to find better semantic matches
• Cross-Encoder Reranking — A dedicated neural model rescores every candidate for precision
• MMR Diversification — Filters redundant chunks to maximize information coverage
• Parent Document Retrieval — Expands matched sentences to their surrounding paragraph context
• Graph Context Packing — Optimizes the 4,096-token budget using graph algorithms
• Lost-in-Middle Mitigation — Reorders evidence so the AI sees the most critical content

DOCUMENT PROCESSING
• Semantic Chunking — Splits by logic and structure, not just character counts
• Multi-Column OCR — Metal-accelerated Vision processing at 360 DPI for complex PDF layouts
• Font Cipher Detection — Detects encoded/garbled text layers and forces full OCR automatically
• Entity Extraction — Names, organizations, technical terms indexed per document

ANTI-HALLUCINATION (7 GATES)
Every answer passes through verification gates A through G: retrieval confidence, evidence coverage, numeric sanity, contradiction detection, semantic grounding, quote faithfulness, and generation quality. Critical failures trigger full abstention — the app refuses to answer rather than answer wrong.

Evidence-First Mode activates when your question barely overlaps with retrieved content. The AI states what evidence exists instead of fabricating a plausible-sounding answer.

THREE QUALITY MODES
• Standard — Fast factual answers in seconds
• Deep Think — 4-8 reasoning sessions with iterative retrieval and cross-validation. Ideal for legal, medical, or academic research
• Maximum — 8-50 sessions with exhaustive multi-chain synthesis for research-grade analysis

AI HUB TRANSFORMS
