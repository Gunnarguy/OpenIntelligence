Ask questions. Get answers grounded in your documents.

OpenIntelligence is a document-aware AI assistant that runs entirely on your device. Import your files, ask questions, and receive answers that cite the exact passages they came from.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Import documents — PDFs, text files, Markdown, code, even images (OCR extracts text automatically).

2. Ask anything — "What does section 3.2 say about pricing?" or "Find all mentions of authentication."

3. Get grounded answers — The AI searches your documents using hybrid retrieval (semantic meaning + keyword matching), then generates responses with inline citations.

RETRIEVAL PIPELINE

Your documents are processed through a production-grade RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline:

• Semantic chunking breaks documents into coherent passages
• CoreML embeddings capture meaning in 384-dimensional vectors
• Hybrid search combines BM25 keyword scoring with vector similarity
• Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges results for better accuracy
• Cross-encoder reranking prioritizes the most relevant passages
• Maximal Marginal Relevance ensures diverse, non-redundant context

KNOWLEDGE CONTAINERS

Organize documents into separate libraries. Each container has its own vector database — your work projects stay separate from personal notes.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE

Answers are generated using Apple's Foundation Models. Processing starts on-device and can extend to Private Cloud Compute when needed — always with end-to-end encryption and zero data retention.

SUPPORTED FILES

• Documents: PDF, TXT, Markdown, RTF
• Images: PNG, JPEG, HEIC (OCR extracts text)
• Code: Swift, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Go, Rust, and 20+ languages
• Data: JSON, YAML, CSV

PRIVACY

• All processing happens on-device by default
• Documents never leave your device unless you enable Private Cloud Compute
• No analytics, no tracking, no third-party AI services
• Full transmission log shows exactly what was sent and when

REQUIREMENTS

• iOS 26.0 or later
• Apple Intelligence enabled (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri)
• iPhone 15 Pro or newer recommended
