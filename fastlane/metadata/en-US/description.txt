OpenIntelligence is a privacy-first AI assistant that keeps your data on your device.

KEY FEATURES

• On-Device Processing
Run AI models locally using Apple Intelligence and MLX. Your conversations and documents never leave your device unless you explicitly choose cloud providers.

• Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Import your documents and let OpenIntelligence find relevant information to answer your questions with context from your own knowledge base.

• Multiple AI Providers
Choose from on-device Apple Foundation Models, local llama.cpp models, or connect to cloud providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini.

• Knowledge Containers
Organize your documents into separate containers for different projects or topics. Each container has its own private vector database.

• Hybrid Search
Combines semantic vector search with keyword matching (BM25) for more accurate retrieval of relevant information.

• Privacy Dashboard
Full transparency into any cloud data transmissions. See exactly what was sent and when.

PRIVACY COMMITMENT

OpenIntelligence is designed with privacy as a core principle:
- All processing happens on-device by default
- Documents are stored locally in encrypted containers
- No analytics or tracking
- Cloud features require explicit consent
- Complete data portability

REQUIREMENTS

- iOS 26.0 or later
- Apple Intelligence support recommended for best on-device experience

OpenIntelligence puts you in control of your AI experience while keeping your data private and secure.
