OpenIntelligence is a research-grade RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) engine built for privacy. Your documents stay on your device. Answers come with citations. Everything runs locally by default with optional Private Cloud Compute—zero retention, full transparency.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Most AI apps send your data to the cloud. OpenIntelligence implements 12 peer-reviewed retrieval techniques on-device, giving you research-lab accuracy without sacrificing privacy.

THE 12 RAG FEATURES

1. Hybrid Search — Combines BM25 keyword matching with vector similarity, fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) for best-of-both retrieval.

2. Cross-Encoder Reranking — Neural reranker scores query-document pairs for precision that basic vector search can't match.

3. MMR Diversification — Maximal Marginal Relevance ensures diverse results, not just the most similar ones.

4. HyDE — Hypothetical Document Embeddings generate an ideal answer first, then search for real documents that match it. 15-25% recall improvement.

5. Parent Document Retrieval — Expands matched chunks to include surrounding context from the same section or page.

6. Contextual Compression — LLM-filters irrelevant sentences from retrieved chunks, saving 40-60% of tokens while improving answer quality.

7. RAPTOR-lite — Multi-level abstraction hierarchy (L0 raw chunks to L1 summaries) for both detailed and high-level retrieval.

8. Query Routing — Automatically classifies queries and routes to the optimal retrieval strategy.

9. Agentic RAG — 12+ Tool functions let the LLM autonomously search, summarize, and analyze your library across multiple reasoning steps.

10. Self-RAG — The model critiques its own retrieval decisions and re-searches when confidence is low.

11. Lost-in-Middle Mitigation — Reorders context to place the most relevant chunks at the beginning and end, where models pay more attention.

12. Fine-Tuned Embeddings — CoreML Sentence Transformer (384-dim) optimized for semantic similarity, with NLEmbedding fallback.
