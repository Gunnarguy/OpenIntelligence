Version 2.1.2

This release improves answer quality, cleans up suggestions, and simplifies plan access.

- More direct source-backed answers when your files clearly support them
- Cleaner suggested and follow-up questions that stay closer to the actual source material
- Better prompt capture and trace exports for answer inspection
- Better import cleanup for messy PDFs, scanned manuals, and chunking edge cases
- Free now includes Maximum up to 3 times per day
- Pro and Lifetime now unlock unlimited Maximum
- Previous paid purchases are now grandfathered into Lifetime access in-app
- Lifetime remains the top-tier capacity plan
- Previous document-pack purchases remain honored, but document packs are no longer sold in-app
