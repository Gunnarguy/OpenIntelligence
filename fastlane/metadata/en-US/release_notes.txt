Version 2.1.2

This release improves grounded answers, cleans up suggestions, and updates plan access around Maximum mode.

- Better grounded answers for manuals, technical PDFs, and source-backed document questions
- Fewer generic dead-end refusals when your documents partially support an answer
- Suggested and follow-up questions now stay closer to the actual source passages and avoid placeholder or self-answering prompts
- Better prompt capture, trace export, and answer review detail for inspecting how a response was produced
- Better cleanup for messy PDFs, multi-column manuals, and chunking edge cases
- Better rendering for links, tables, and answer review surfaces
- Free now includes Maximum up to 3 times per day
- Pro and Lifetime now unlock unlimited Maximum
- Prior paid purchases, including legacy document packs, are grandfathered into Lifetime access in-app
- Document packs remain honored for prior purchasers, but new document-pack sales are retired in-app
