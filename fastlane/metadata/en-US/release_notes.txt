Version 3.3

What's new in 3.3:

- Large imports are more resilient. OpenIntelligence now preserves the import queue, resumes interrupted work more cleanly, and shows clearer progress during long-running document processing.
- PDFs and images now use one adaptive visual-ingestion path instead of a manual fidelity setting, so garbled, table-heavy, image-heavy, and small-text pages get stronger recovery automatically.
- Embedded PDF figures and standalone images are now preserved as searchable evidence with captions, labels, and nearby context instead of being easier to lose during ingestion.
- Exact spec and table lookups are stronger, and suggested starter questions stay closer to what your current library can actually answer cleanly.

This update is focused on making imports and grounded answers more trustworthy again.
