Expedited review request for OpenIntelligence 3.5

We are requesting an expedited review because the live 3.2.5 line can regress in the app's core document Q&A flow. On dense PDFs and table-heavy documents, direct source-backed questions can surface degraded or mixed passages, and some long-running imports are less reliable after interruption. This affects the primary workflow users open the app for.

Version 3.5 is the corrective release. It improves exact-value answer quality for source-backed questions, reduces PDF and table-ingestion contamination, and improves long-running import reliability.

Simple reproduction on the live version:
1. Import a dense PDF or table-heavy technical document.
2. Ask a direct question about a value, measurement, date, count, limit, or table row.
3. Optionally background the app during a longer import.
4. In some cases, answer quality or completion reliability degrades.

We would appreciate an expedited review because this update is intended to correct a live regression in the app's primary user flow.
