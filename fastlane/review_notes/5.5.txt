New in 5.5: a "how much" question only looks for a volume when it asks about something measured that way, such as fuel, oil, coolant or a tank, so it no longer answers with a weight from an unrelated document. Pro Monthly and Pro Annual unlock Pro while the subscription is active, and the Lifetime purchase unlocks Lifetime. Private Cloud Compute is Apple's, and the app uses it only after the consent sheet is approved.

Plans screen: under each price it now shows what Pro Annual saves against twelve months of Pro Monthly, Pro Annual's price per month, and whether Lifetime costs less than a year of Pro Monthly. Each figure is computed from the storefront's own StoreKit prices and sits below the billed amount in a smaller size; the billed amount is the largest price on every card. The Lifetime purchase is now named "Lifetime" (it was "Lifetime Cohort"); the product identifier is unchanged. Prices change on September 30 (Lifetime $49.99) and October 1 (Pro Annual $24.99, Pro Monthly $4.99), so the screen may show either set depending on the review date.
