New in 5.8, with no new purchases or permissions. On the Mac the window has a sidebar listing Chat, Documents, Atlas and Database, the libraries and the open library's conversations, and Settings opens in its own window from OpenIntelligence > Settings or Command-comma. iPhone and iPad keep their layout. The app's three guides are in their own OpenIntelligence Guide library, which can't be deleted, takes no new files and counts toward no plan limit; the person's first library is now named Library 1 (it was General). On Pro and Lifetime a library can watch a folder the person picks; the app only reads the files in it. The plans screen adds a table comparing Free, Pro and Lifetime. On iPhone and iPad, new capability: Background Inference, so an import the person started can keep using the Neural Engine after they leave the app. App links now use gunndamental.com. No account is needed to test: open the app, choose the OpenIntelligence Guide library, ask a question in Chat and tap a citation to open its passage. Private Cloud Compute is Apple's, and the app uses it only after the consent sheet is approved.
