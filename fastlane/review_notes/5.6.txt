New in 5.6: fixes to how an answer is shown, with no new features, purchases or permissions. Citations in an answer such as [S1] now open the cited passage when tapped. Answers keep numbers and times as the document wrote them ("$300.00", "10:00 p.m."). The Verified badge now shows only when the app's checks ran and passed; otherwise an answer reads Unverified or Not Checked. In Deep Think and Maximum, choosing "Just Once" on the Private Cloud Compute consent sheet now covers one question, so the sheet is shown again for the next one. A device that cannot run Apple Intelligence, or has it turned off or still downloading, is told which and what still works. The Documents tab no longer asks for a search index rebuild on a library that does not need one. Private Cloud Compute is Apple's, and the app uses it only after the consent sheet is approved.
