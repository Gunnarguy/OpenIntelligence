No login, account, or demo credentials are required.

Suggested review path:
1. Launch the app and complete onboarding.
2. Let the bundled sample documents import.
3. In Standard mode, ask one of these sample questions: "How does OpenIntelligence work around the 4,096-token limit?" or "What file types does OpenIntelligence handle best?"
4. Open response details or source cards to inspect the supporting passages.

Standard is the fast grounded mode. Deep Think and Maximum are the separate higher-effort modes.

The app works offline by default. Core parsing, OCR, indexing, and retrieval are local-first. Generation uses Apple Foundation Models where available.
