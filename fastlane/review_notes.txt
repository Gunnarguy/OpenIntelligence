No login required. No account creation. No demo credentials needed. The app works offline.

APP OVERVIEW:
OpenIntelligence is an on-device RAG engine. Users import documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, CSV, RTF, TXT, images), the app indexes them locally, and then answers natural-language questions with cited sources. Core AI uses Apple Foundation Models on-device, with PCC used only when iOS decides it is needed.

KEY FEATURES:
- Grounded document Q&A with citations and source review
- 3 quality modes: Standard, Deep Think, Maximum
- Live reasoning strip in the unified bar during deeper reasoning
- Verification and answer-sanitization to reduce unsupported claims
- AI Hub transforms grounded in imported source material
- Library isolation so answers do not leak across libraries

TEST STEPS:
1. Launch app, complete onboarding, and let the 3 sample documents import.
2. Ask: "What are the pricing tiers?" or "How does the RAG pipeline work?"
3. In Standard mode, ask a question, then tap "Go Deeper" and verify the app stays responsive while the deeper answer streams.
4. In Deep Think mode, run two deeper queries in the same thread and verify the app remains responsive.
5. During Deep Think or Maximum, verify the unified bar under the library and quality selectors shows the current live reasoning step.
6. Ask a longer question in Deep Think and verify the final answer does not end mid-bullet or mid-thought.
7. Import a dense PDF or table-heavy document and verify answers stay grounded and cite the right values.
8. Test AI Hub transforms after an answer.
9. Create a second library and verify answers do not leak between libraries.

PRIVACY: Processing is local by default using Apple Foundation Models. No third-party AI services. No analytics. No tracking.
