No login required. No account creation. Core document import and Q&A work without sign-in.

APP OVERVIEW:
OpenIntelligence is a local-first document Q&A app. Users import documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, CSV, RTF, TXT, images), the app indexes them locally, and then answers questions from those files with citations and source review. Core functionality remains available offline.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.1.2:
- Improved source-backed answering for manuals, technical documents, and dense PDFs
- Cleaner suggested questions and follow-up questions that stay closer to the uploaded source material
- Better trace export and prompt capture for answer inspection
- Free tier now includes Maximum mode up to 3 uses per day
- Pro Monthly / Pro Annual unlock unlimited Maximum plus larger quotas
- Lifetime unlocks unlimited Maximum, unlimited documents, and 10 libraries
- Prior paid purchases, including legacy document packs, are grandfathered into Lifetime access in-app
- Document packs are legacy-only: the app no longer offers new document-pack purchases in-app

KEY FEATURES:
- Source-backed answers with citations and source review
- Three quality modes: Standard, Deep Think, Maximum
- Suggested questions and follow-up questions grounded in uploaded documents
- Multi-column OCR and cleanup for difficult manuals and noisy PDFs
- AI Hub transforms such as Key Facts, Step-by-Step, Plain English, Gap Analysis, and Illustrate

IN-APP PURCHASES:
- Free: 5 documents, 1 library, Standard and Deep Think available, Maximum limited to 3 uses per day
- Pro Monthly ($5.99): unlimited Maximum, 1,000 documents, 5 libraries
- Pro Annual ($49.99): same unlocks as monthly, annual billing
- Lifetime ($59.99): one-time, unlimited Maximum, unlimited documents, 10 libraries
- Legacy Document Pack: prior purchases are grandfathered into Lifetime access; intentionally hidden from the new purchase UI

TEST STEPS:
1. Launch app and complete onboarding
2. Ask a question against the sample documents or import your own file from Files
3. Open the mode selector and verify Standard, Deep Think, and Maximum are available
4. On free tier, use Maximum three times; on the fourth attempt, the app shows upgrade and fallback options instead of running Maximum
5. Purchase or restore Pro / Lifetime, then verify Maximum becomes unlimited
6. Import a PDF or manual and ask a grounded question; citations and source review remain available
7. Create a second library and verify answers do not leak across libraries
8. Document packs are not shown for new purchase because they are legacy-only by design

PRIVACY:
All document import, storage, indexing, and core Q&A stay local. Apple-managed Private Cloud Compute may be used only through Apple system features and device settings when the platform routes a request there. No third-party AI services. No analytics. No tracking.
