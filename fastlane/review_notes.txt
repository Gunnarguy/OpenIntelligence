Version 3.2.5 is a corrective follow-up to 3.2. The last updates were ambitious and improved much of the document reasoning pipeline, but they also made some simple source-backed questions slower or less reliable than they should have been. Sorry for the back-to-back submission. This build restores the intended behavior: when an exact answer is present in an uploaded document, the app should find and cite it directly, while Deep Think and Maximum are still available for genuinely harder questions.

No login required. No account creation. No demo credentials needed. The app works offline.

Suggested review path:
1. Launch the app and complete onboarding.
2. Let the included sample documents import, or import any PDF/table-heavy document.
3. Ask a specific exact-value question, such as a count, price, measurement, date, limit, or value from a table.
4. Try the same style of question in Standard, Deep Think, and Maximum.
5. Verify the answer is concise, cited, and does not require unnecessary long reasoning when the source value is clear.

Privacy: Processing is local by default using Apple Foundation Models where available. No third-party AI services by default. No analytics. No tracking.
