# Target Account Framework

This is the sheet for building the first real company list.
Use it with Dave tomorrow so you do not waste time chasing bad-fit accounts.

## Goal

Build a first list of `20` companies that are plausible design partners for the current founder-led evaluation path.

Do not build a giant list.
Build a sharp list.

## Best-Fit Company Shape

Prioritize companies that have:

- Apple-native product surface
- document-heavy workflows
- high trust requirements
- user value tied to getting the right answer, not just any answer
- willingness to run a pilot instead of demanding a perfect self-serve SDK on day one

## Best-Fit Use Cases

Strong early use cases:

- field service and technician support
- product manuals and installation guidance
- internal enablement and training
- healthcare or regulated operations knowledge
- legal, compliance, or case-based document review
- enterprise mobile workflows where iPhone or iPad already matters

## Bad-Fit Company Shape

Avoid companies that require:

- cross-platform web SDK first
- hosted API instead of embedded logic
- immediate enterprise procurement with no pilot
- mass self-serve rollout before founder involvement
- low-trust consumer chat behavior instead of evidence-aware answers

## Who To Contact

Best first roles:

- founder
- CTO
- head of product
- head of mobile
- GM of a document-heavy workflow product
- VP or director of support / operations for mobile-heavy tools

## Scoring Criteria

Score each account from `1-5` on:

- Apple fit
- document pain
- trust / privacy need
- urgency
- founder access

Total score:

- `20-25`: top-priority outreach now
- `15-19`: good second wave
- `10-14`: only if intro is warm
- `<10`: skip for now

## Research Signals

Look for these signals:

- iPhone or iPad app is core to the workflow
- product mentions manuals, SOPs, policies, knowledge base, cases, or records
- privacy, offline, or on-device positioning matters
- support or operations teams are overloaded by document search
- company is small enough to move fast but large enough to pay for a pilot

## First 20 Mix

Use this rough mix:

- `6` vertical SaaS teams with document-heavy workflows
- `4` field-service or industrial-support tools
- `4` healthcare / legal / compliance / regulated workflow tools
- `3` enterprise mobile-product teams with strong Apple surface area
- `3` wildcard accounts with a warm intro or unusually strong fit
