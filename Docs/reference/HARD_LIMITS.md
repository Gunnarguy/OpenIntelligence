# Public Product Constraints

This file documents only the public-facing constraints that matter for understanding OpenIntelligence as a product. Detailed engine limits and internal safety thresholds are intentionally kept private.

## Product Constraints

- iOS 26.0 or later is required.
- Apple Intelligence availability depends on device, region, language support, and Apple platform state.
- Larger or lower-quality documents may take longer to prepare.
- Some Apple-managed cloud behavior may depend on user settings and platform routing outside the app's direct control.
- Performance varies by device class, file type, and document quality.

## Public Guidance

- Use the latest iOS release available for your device.
- Expect imported content to prepare faster on newer hardware.
- Treat cited answers as a productivity tool, then verify against the linked source passages when accuracy matters.

## Private Detail Boundary

Exact engine budgets, thresholds, validation rules, and optimization limits are not published in the public repository.
