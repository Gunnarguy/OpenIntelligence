# Ten lines fix two of eleven links

**Yes, the proposed change is a band-aid, though a correct one worth keeping.** It removes two of at least eleven decisions that turned "How much notice do I have to give before I move out?" into "1 lb." labelled Verified. One is the rule that pads every "much/many" question with volume words. The other is the substring test that let " l" inside "1 lb" pass as a liquid unit [code_verified, exact; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:1482-1484`, `:587`]. By the extractor's own constants, that stops its candidate path from locking any plain "how much / how many" question, not just this one, because such a question can now score at most 0.80 against a 0.82 lock threshold [inferred (arithmetic on code constants), medium-high; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:461-563`, `EvidenceScoringPolicyService.swift:82-84`]. The failure class stays open, though. Hand-written code can still answer in place of the model, nothing checks that an answer is the kind of thing the question asked for, and the app still labels such answers Verified. AFM 3 did not cause this. In Deep Think the question never reached it for an answer, and the only model call was the execution planner [inferred, high; `RAGService.swift:8904-8918`]. Nor is the retrieval architecture the "much larger thing". Your own chunker, a MiniLM embedder, SQLite FTS5 beside flat vector files, fused hybrid search, a cross-encoder and a packed prompt into the on-device model follow the chunk, embed, store, retrieve and feed recipe that Apple's TN3193 describes [documented, [TN3193](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3193-managing-the-on-device-foundation-model-s-context-window), fetched 2026-09-26], in the shape that open-source systems ship [primary, open-source defaults compared below]. The larger problem sits around that architecture, in three parts. Heuristics pre-empt or overwrite the model's answer. Verification checks presence rather than whether the question was answered, and never runs in Deep Think or Maximum. Plumbing defects discard the ranking the pipeline computes and let chunks drop out of retrieval. Ten changes would be fundamental, and they are ranked at the end by effect on answer correctness. None of them, the ten lines included, can be proven without a labelled question set over leases, manuals and receipts, because almost every retrieval-quality number in the repository comes from research papers [artifact_derived, high; `BenchmarkRuns/LEDGER.md`].

### What the ten lines are still worth

The two changed links are real defects, and fixing them reaches further than this one sentence. Take any "how much" or "how many" question that contains no measurement word (such as amount, size or weight), no fluid or container word (oil, fuel, coolant, tank) and no primary entity. With both changes in place, it can score at most 0.25 + 0.15 + 0.10 + 0.10 + 0.10 + 0.10 = **0.80** on the candidate path, below the 0.82 lock. The whole family of plain quantity questions, such as notice periods, rent, deadlines and headcounts, can therefore no longer lock on the candidate path. Only the separate table phases, covered below, can still lock them [inferred (arithmetic on code constants), medium-high; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:461-563`, `:566-581`].

Exact unit matching also stops a weight from answering a genuine fuel question, and it recognises "4.5L" written without a space [inferred (port of the proposed helper), medium]. In the port, current code fails three of four regression cases. It returns "1 lb" for the notice question against a cooking chart, "5.8 qt" for the same question against a spec page, and "3,200 lb" for "How much fuel does the tank hold?". The diff passes all four and still locks the correct "5.8 qt" for "How much food does the basket hold?". Each half of the diff on its own leaves one case failing [inferred (port), medium].

The deterministic keyword order removes a misleading diagnostic. `Array(Set(...))` shuffled keywords per process, which made the incident's log read as if the expansion had replaced the question's own words [code_verified, exact; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:1499`, `:1509`]. The fixtures are the extractive path's first regression tests and are worth keeping whatever else changes.

### Why it is still a band-aid

The nine unchanged links are general mechanisms rather than quirks of this sentence, and that is what makes the diff a band-aid [inferred, high]. Five examples show how far they reach.

**Measurement words still trigger the path.** A question containing amount, size, weight, temperature, pressure or length is still a measurement query. An "amount" question can reach 0.87 with no unit check at all [inferred (arithmetic), medium-high; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:566-581`]. The weight and size unit tests also still match substrings such as "g ", " m" and "in" [code_verified, exact; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:594-606`].

**The table phases are untouched.** The structured-table and state-mapping phases score separately (+0.50, +0.30, +0.20), and the diff does not change them [code_verified, exact; `SpecificationExtractor.swift:1288-1362`]. In the port, "How much oil does the engine take?" asked of "Oil capacity with filter change: 4.5 L (4.8 qt). Use SAE 0W-20 synthetic oil." locks **"0W-20"**, an oil grade, at 0.85, both before and after the diff [inferred (port), medium].

**The sniper still ranks the wrong pages first.** On a fuller fictional library, the port's sniper ranked two air-fryer pages above the lease page. The air-fryer pages matched only "move" and "out" (scores 0.835 and 0.805); the lease page contained all four query words (0.750). Current code then locked "5.8 qt", a different wrong answer that only the expansion half prevents [inferred (port), medium].

**The precision-value classifier over-matches.** Its unit pattern matches the words "a", "m", "g", "v" and "w", so "What is a security deposit?" forces an override attempt at the 0.90 threshold [code_verified (port of the literal lists), high; `RAGService.swift:18149-18189`].

**One flag gates it all.** A single heuristic intent flag controls the sniper, the extractive re-sort, the override, the source-only check and a halved abstention threshold. It is read 17 times in RAGService.swift and 9 in RetrievalPolicyService.swift, plus 25 reads through `answerIntentIsExtractive` [grep_verified, high]. The query path carries about **300 hand-written string lists holding 5,100 literals, 240 regex call sites and 26 distinct stopword sets** [grep_verified (script), medium]. The audit found no test or benchmark that measures how precise any of them are [grep_verified, medium].

### What would close the incident

Under the repository's own rule, a fix closes nothing until it is verified where the defect appeared. For this diff that means Deep Think on the same library.

**A Deep Think re-ask exercises the fix.** Deep Think dispatches at `RAGService.swift:9544`, before the Standard path's exact-cache lookup at `:9834`, so a re-ask there runs the new extractor [code_verified for line order, exact; that no agentic step reads the cache is inferred, medium-high].

**A Standard re-ask may not.** A Standard-mode re-ask of an earlier question can be answered from cached chunks. An exact semantic-cache hit skips rewriting, embedding and search, and nothing ever deletes from `semantic_query_cache` or removes `fulltext.sqlite` [code_verified, confirmed twice; `RAGService.swift:9832-9850`; `SQLiteFullTextService.swift:3314-3435`].

## Retrieval follows Apple's recipe while heuristics hold the answer seat

### Ingestion turns every file into word-counted text chunks

Every file enters through `RAGService.addDocument` and `DocumentProcessor.processDocument`. PDFs over 10 MB take a streamed path in 15-page batches [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:5607`, `:5939-5959`].

**Parsing.** PDF pages are rendered at 5-6x scale (about 360-432 DPI) and read with Vision's `RecognizeDocumentsRequest`. When a page already has a layout text layer, the app takes its PDFKit text instead [code_verified, exact for configuration, medium for routing; `StructuredDocumentParser.swift:689`; `DocumentProcessor.swift:219-244`, `:4326-4328`]. Within a page, the parser emits the title first, then every table, every list and every paragraph, in that order rather than in reading order. Figures are appended after the last page, so each one inherits the last section's title [code_verified, exact; `StructuredDocumentParser.swift:807-915`; `DocumentProcessor.swift:5045-5048`]. Office files are unzipped and read with regular expressions over their XML. iWork files are not parsed at all, and audio language is guessed from the filename [code_verified, exact; `DocumentProcessor.swift:8872-9380`, `:8839-8845`, `:8783-8797`].

**Chunking.** The chunker counts words, not tokens. Its effective target is at most **260 words**, with a hard maximum of 310 and at most 50 words of overlap, because DocumentProcessor clamps every request. As a result, the self-tuned windows of 280-420 words never take effect [code_verified, exact; `SemanticChunker.swift:156-174`; `DocumentProcessor.swift:858-884`]. A second guard splits any chunk over 430 tokens. It breaks the text at every ".", "!", "?" and newline and rejoins the parts with ". ", **which turns "4.5 L" into "4. 5 L"** [code_verified, confirmed twice; `DocumentProcessor.swift:6440`, `:6603`, `:6647`].

**Embedding.** Each chunk is embedded once with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions, a 512-token input, mean pooling). A "[file] [section path] " prefix is added first, and the text is truncated to 512 tokens by a character-level binary search [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:6291-6359`; `CoreMLSentenceEmbeddingProvider.swift:64-66`, `:113`; model identity doc_claim_only, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md:3-6`]. On OS 27, Settings and container creation assign the Core AI export of the same model; the conditions around that assignment were not read [grep_verified, medium]. That runtime's output is not L2-normalised in Swift [code_verified, exact; `CoreAISentenceEmbeddingProvider.swift:210-233`].

### Storage splits text and vectors across two stores that share no key

**Text store.** All libraries share one SQLite file, `fulltext.sqlite` under Application Support. It holds three FTS5 tables (documents, chunks and pages) tokenised with `porter unicode61`, four ordinary tables, and a persistent query cache. Libraries are separated only by a `container_id` column, which is UNINDEXED inside every FTS5 table [code_verified, exact; `SQLiteFullTextService.swift:84-95`, `:169-350`]. At all four bm25 call sites, the chunk index weights section title at 10, section path at 5 and body at 1, so the earlier eight-weight defect is fixed [code_verified, exact; `SQLiteFullTextService.swift:1440-1473`].

**Vector store.** Vectors live outside SQLite, in three files per library. `_vectors.bin` is raw Float32, memory-mapped, with no header, dimension or checksum. `_norms.bin` holds the norms. `_meta.json` holds every chunk's text and metadata and is decoded whole into memory. Search is an exhaustive cosine scan using Accelerate vDSP or Metal; despite the class name, no BNNS API is called [code_verified, exact; `BNNSVectorDatabase.swift:119-139`, `:143-282`, `:307-337`, `:486-607`].

**No shared identifier.** SQLite keys chunk rows `"<documentId>_<chunkIndex>"`, while vector chunks carry random UUIDs. Hybrid search joins the two on (documentId, chunkIndex) and silently drops any keyword hit it cannot resolve [code_verified, exact; `SQLiteFullTextService.swift:864`; `HybridSearchService.swift:≈1071-1128`].

**No transaction.** Ingestion writes document text, then vectors, then chunk rows, then a summary chunk. No transaction spans those writes, and nothing rolls back on failure [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:6506-6732`, `:6965-7018`].

**Sync.** SQLite never leaves the device, but vector files are copied into the iCloud container without file coordination [code_verified, exact; `WorkspaceSyncService.swift:272-278`, `:2660-2674`].

### AFM 3 plans, writes and reasons, but decides almost nothing

AFM 3 enters at three points. It makes a planning call. It writes the answer in Standard, or runs the reasoning sessions in Deep Think and Maximum. And it makes two calls for a source-only check on extractive questions whose gates failed [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:14733-14750`, `:17290-17293`]. Word lists and scoring rules, not the model, decide what kind of question it is, which evidence goes first, whether the model's answer stands, and what label the user sees [inferred from code_verified call sites, high].

**The token budget uses a fixed ratio.** The app reads `SystemLanguageModel.contextSize` live, then converts the window to characters at a fixed **1.4 characters per token**, a ratio with no recorded source, computes the evidence budget before the system prompt is assigned, and hard-codes 4,096 in the agentic audit [code_verified, exact; `FoundationModelTokenBudget.swift:26-54`; `RAGService.swift:13529`, `:9110-9114`]. The exact `tokenCount(for:)` path runs only in the routing planner [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:16290-16300`].

**What that ratio produces.** For the incident question on a 4,096-token window, the budget works out to (4,096 − 120 − 38 − 300) × 0.88 × 1.4 ≈ **4,481 characters of evidence**. It sits beside a system prompt of about 2,200-2,600 characters that the budget never counts [inferred (arithmetic on code constants), high; template measured at `RAGService.swift:13455-13536`]. At Apple's documented 3-4 characters per token, the evidence fills about a third of a 4,096-token window. At the app's own 1.4, the full prompt would overflow it [inferred, medium; ratio documented in [Managing the context window](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/managing-the-context-window), fetched 2026-09-26]. Deep Think's per-session windows are fixed at 4,000 and 2,200 characters, whatever the device offers [code_verified, exact; `AgenticOrchestrator.swift:8011-8062`].

**Reasoning levels.** They go only to Private Cloud Compute (PCC). That matches Apple's capability table, which lists on-device reasoning as not supported [code_verified, exact; `FoundationModelRoute.swift:48-99`; documented, [PCC article](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/adding-server-side-intelligence-with-private-cloud-compute), fetched 2026-09-26]. Deep Think's chain sessions, however, stay on-device unless the user prefers PCC [code_verified, exact; `AgenticOrchestrator.swift:9008-9014`].

**Deep Think varies by device.** The device tier sets the chain length, the quality bands and whether the Self-RAG check runs. On an A17 Pro tier, that check never runs [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:8872-8880`; `AgenticOrchestrator.swift:780`].

**No model-version awareness.** Nothing in `Services` checks `SystemLanguageModel.variant` or the OS model version [grep_verified, medium]. Yet iOS 27 ships two on-device variants, `.core3` ("AFM 3 Core") and `.coreAdvanced3` ("AFM 3 Core Advanced"), and Apple tells developers to re-test prompts whenever an OS update changes the model [documented, [Variant](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/systemlanguagemodel/variant-swift.struct) and [Foundation Models updates](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/updates/foundationmodels), fetched 2026-09-26]. Apple's developer pages publish no parameter counts. Press summaries of Apple's research post, which this environment could not fetch, describe Core as a 3B dense model. They describe Core Advanced as a 20B sparse model with 1-4B active parameters, running only on 12 GB devices [inferred (search summary), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/11/apples-new-foundation-models-explained-on-device-ai-cloud-ai-and-everything-in-between/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/phones/ios/only-3-iphones-can-access-the-best-version-of-siri-ai-heres-which-features-are-exclusive-to-apples-most-powerful-on-device-model-afm-core-advanced)].

## The shape is standard, and the defects undo work the pipeline already did

Sorting the findings into four bins answers the band-aid question more precisely than yes or no. The system's shape is the one the field builds, and two of its components are a generation behind. The defects cluster in hand-written stages placed after the trained ones and in storage plumbing, and several of them discard work the pipeline has already done. The missing capabilities are mostly the checks that current practice treats as the guard against exactly this failure.

**1. Context size.** No Apple source puts 32K on the device; 32K is the PCC model's window [documented, [PCC article](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/adding-server-side-intelligence-with-private-cloud-compute), fetched 2026-09-26]. Apple's instruction is to use `contextSize` and `tokenCount(for:)` "to adapt your app to the hardware it's running on" [documented, [session 241](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/241/), fetched 2026-09-26]. Reading 8,192 as the Core Advanced value and 4,096 as the Core floor is an inference Apple has not confirmed [inferred, low-medium]. Reading the window live does not repair the packing, for three reasons: Standard's character budget is capped at 10,000 characters however large the window, Deep Think's session windows stay at 4,000 and 2,200 characters, and the system prompt stays uncounted on every device [code_verified, exact for the cap and the session windows; that the Standard budget starts from the live window is inferred, medium-high; `RAGService.swift:12720-12730`; `AgenticOrchestrator.swift:8011-8062`]. The fix is to count the real prompt with `tokenCount(for:)`, not to pick a better ratio.

**2. Adapters.** The training lever that the small-model literature recommends is closed for AFM 3. This was not confirmed against the iOS 27 SDK interface from this host [documented, [adapter toolkit](https://developer.apple.com/apple-intelligence/foundation-models-adapter/), fetched 2026-09-26]. What remains is retrieval quality, prompts, guided generation and answer gates. A different model behind the new `LanguageModel` protocol, such as a Core AI or MLX model, is also possible, but that is a separate product decision [documented, [LanguageModel](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/languagemodel), fetched 2026-09-26].

**3. Chunk length, recomputed from the code's real limits.** English text averages about 1.25-1.3 WordPiece tokens per word [inferred (search summary), [FinNLP 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.finnlp-1.9.pdf)]. A 260-word chunk is therefore about 325-340 tokens, and a 310-word chunk about 390-400, before the prefix. Technical text, at the chunker's own estimate of 1.3-2.0 tokens per word, reaches 400-620 tokens, which is where the 430-token guard splits and rewrites [code_verified comment, `SemanticChunker.swift:139-143`; inferred, high]. The app runs MiniLM with a 512-token input, so the survey's conclusion that each chunk's tail never reaches its vector does not apply here. What does apply is that the chunks are roughly 2.5 to 4 times the 128-token length the model was fine-tuned on [inferred (search summary), [model card](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2)]. They also exceed the 256-token default under which its published scores were produced [primary, [MTEB registry](https://pypi.org/pypi/mteb/json)]. The only direct evidence is weak. One hub discussion reported NDCG@10 of 0.51262 at 128 tokens, 0.47246 at 256 and 0.46083 at 512, on an unnamed test [inferred (search summary), [discussion 54](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/discussions/54)]. Chroma's rerun with MiniLM found the best recall at 250-token chunks [inferred (search summary), [Chroma](https://www.trychroma.com/research/evaluating-chunking)]. The effect on this app's retrieval is unmeasured.

**4. Contextual retrieval.** The deterministic header is the cheap half of Anthropic's method. The expensive half is 50-100 tokens of LLM-written context per chunk, which cut top-20 retrieval failures by 35% with embeddings alone and by 49% with contextual BM25 added [primary, [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval)]. The app's section part is also thinner than it looks. It is dropped whenever the label is not "trusted", it is a single flat page title in structured PDFs, and it names the wrong section for figures. The share of chunks carrying only a filename is unknown [code_verified, exact; `RAGService.swift:2984-3010`; `DocumentProcessor.swift:5250-5256`, `:5045-5048`].

**5. `ContextOptions`.** The app passes route-derived options at five call sites and shipped, so this is a documentation correction, not a defect [code_verified, exact; `LLMService.swift:879-884`, `:1464-1469`; `FoundationModelStructuredGenerator.swift:84-89`, `:132-137`, `:252-254`]. Its practical consequence is that call paths compatible with iOS 26 cannot carry a reasoning level.

**6. The 452 chunks.** They might surface through the page-level or document-level text-search paths, which is unsettled [inferred, high; `RAGService.swift:18427`, `:20060`]. Which of five silent drop paths removed them is also unsettled. The owner measured `_norms.bin` directly, so the figure does not depend on `runVectorAudit`, which would count every chunk as missing on this store [code_verified, high; `RAGService.swift:504-507`].

## Ten changes would be fundamental, led by the answer seat

### No benchmark yet covers leases or the precision path

No benchmark in the repository covers leases or other household documents, or measures how often the precision path locks on a wrong answer [artifact_derived, high; `LEDGER.md`]. The remedy is a golden set of about 50-150 questions over a fixed library of the owner's document types [inferred, medium; method from [Anthropic's statistical-evals guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/research/statistical-approach-to-model-evals), primary]. Each question would carry its gold chunk IDs, expected answer type, gold answer and whether it is answerable. The set would be seeded with deliberate traps, such as a lease question where only an air-fryer manual contains a number. Per mode, it would report recall and nDCG for each retrieval arm, answer correctness, abstention precision and recall, and a false-"Verified" rate, with errors clustered by document.

Apple's Evaluations framework runs datasets like this under Swift Testing on iOS/macOS 27, with model judges and a groundedness criterion [documented, [Evaluations](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/evaluations), fetched 2026-09-26]. Every change below depends on this set to be proven. It is the owner's roadmap row [Adopt Apple's Evaluations framework for answer-quality grading](https://app.notion.com/3b549a74d54f814cbb06fa629417b657) (Future Backlog).

### Why each change sits where it does

**1. The answer seat ranks first because it produced this failure and nothing else counterbalances it.** An extractive reader with no answerability decision is exactly the failure SQuAD 2.0 was built to expose [inferred (search summary), [arXiv 1806.03822](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.03822)], and none of the surveyed open-source systems replaces a generated answer with a regex match [inferred (absence in the source files read across RAGFlow, LlamaIndex, Haystack, txtai, Onyx and others), medium]. Guided generation writes fields in their declared order, so an evidence-first schema makes the model quote before it claims, and code can then check that the quote is verbatim in the cited chunk and that its unit fits the question [documented, [guided generation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/generating-swift-data-structures-with-guided-generation), fetched 2026-09-26]. The shortcut does exist for real wins and costs one model call where the orchestrator costs nine or more, but its false-lock rate has never been measured [unknown; inferred, medium], so the trade-off is the owner's to weigh.

**2. The ranking comes next because its costs are measured.** The rank-1 loss after reranking, and fusion losing to its own keyword arm, are recorded in the ledger above. About 40 labelled queries are enough for a tuned weighted fusion to start beating RRF [inferred (search summary), [Elastic](https://www.elastic.co/search-labs/blog/improving-information-retrieval-elastic-stack-hybrid)], and because a cross-encoder's raw score is not a calibrated relevance probability, its thresholds belong on the golden set [inferred (search summary), [arXiv 2411.11767](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11767)].

**3. Verification changes what the user sees for every answer, not only extracted ones.** Small checkers exist for the entailment half: MiniCheck matched GPT-4 at 770M parameters [inferred (search summary), [EMNLP 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.499/)] and TinyLettuce ships 17-68M models [primary, [LettuceDetect](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KRLabsOrg/LettuceDetect/main/README.md)], though their on-device latency is unmeasured. Honest labels come from a check that ran, keep "not evaluated" apart from "supported", and attach to claims. That matters because users trust citations even when they are random [inferred (search summary), [arXiv 2501.01303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01303)], and Apple's HIG warns against conveying confidence whose link to quality is unknown [documented, [HIG: Machine learning](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/machine-learning), fetched 2026-09-26]. The label half changes no answer; it changes whether a wrong one is trusted.

**4. The embedder is the largest quality lever the field has measured.** Same-size models score 8-10 points more on BEIR, and 150M-600M models 13-14 more [primary, [Arctic README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Snowflake-Labs/arctic-embed/main/README.md); raw MTEB results], and they are already available to Swift through the MLXEmbedders registry [primary, [ModelFactory.swift](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-swift-lm/blob/main/Libraries/MLXEmbedders/ModelFactory.swift)]. The switch needs a migration that never mixes two models' vectors, because the vector files record neither the model nor the dimension [code_verified, exact; `BNNSVectorDatabase.swift:307-337`].

**5. Store consistency is severe where it strikes.** One library has 24% of its chunks unreachable, and the loader can wipe a library's vectors, though how often either happens is unknown [measured by the owner; confirmed twice for the loader]. Apple's archived iCloud guide makes file coordination mandatory for iCloud documents [documented, [archived guide](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/General/Conceptual/iCloudDesignGuide/Chapters/DesigningForDocumentsIniCloud.html), fetched 2026-09-26], and `CKSyncEngine` is Apple's current engine for syncing record data [documented, [CKSyncEngine](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/cloudkit/cksyncengine-4b4w9.json), fetched 2026-09-26].

**6. Chunk fidelity comes next because damaged text reaches every answer path.** The extractive path, which quotes chunk text verbatim, is the most exposed. Current practice sizes chunks with the embedder's own tokenizer and caps them at its window [primary, [Docling HybridChunker](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/docling-project/docling-core/HEAD/docling_core/transforms/chunker/hybrid_chunker.py)]. The size of the gain here is unmeasured.

**7. The budget change is cheap but moderate in effect.** On non-literal questions, 4-8B models fall below half their short-context accuracy by 2K-8K tokens [primary, [NoLiMa](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-research/NoLiMa/main/README.md)]. The gain is exact accounting and better-chosen passages, not simply more passages.

**8. The cache change is small but gates verification of everything above.** Without it, an on-device check of any Standard-mode fix can replay retrieval computed by older code [inferred, high].

**9. The lexical arm is the app's strongest, and fixing it means a destructive rebuild.** FTS5 computes bm25 document counts and lengths over the whole shared table [primary, [fts5_aux.c](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sqlite/sqlite/master/ext/fts5/fts5_aux.c)]. One index held 4,077 document IDs for about 15 documents [measured per code comment, medium; `OpenIntelligenceRuntimePaths.swift:44-47`].

**10. Deep Think's shape is sound; its gaps matter for multi-hop and whole-library questions.** Accuracy plateaus at around three searches [inferred (search summary), [arXiv 2603.08877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08877)], recursive summaries amplify hallucinations unless they are re-grounded in source passages [inferred (search summary), [arXiv 2502.00977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00977)], and a small model should not choose whether to search, a choice Deep Think's planner call currently makes [inferred (search summary), [arXiv 2604.04651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04651); code_verified, exact; `AgenticOrchestrator.swift:3509-3527`].

## Evidence limits

**Nothing was compiled, run or tested on a device.** The work ran in a Linux container with no Swift toolchain and no iPhone, iPad or Mac, so every app claim comes from reading the source at commit b37ab4c (merged 2026-09-25). SQLite behaviour was reproduced with Python's sqlite3 3.45.1 rather than Apple's build, and extractor behaviour was checked with a Python port on fictional fixtures (`port/port_run_fixture.txt` and `port/port_validate_proposed_test.txt`), which reflects the Swift only if it is faithful and does not port the state-mapping phases.

**History is short.** The git clone is shallow: 54 commits, the earliest 3cce8b3 on 2026-09-19. Nothing older can be dated, including when the fusion weights moved from the ledger's 0.7/0.3 to today's 0.50/0.50, or which on-device model the 1.4 ratio and the prompts were tuned against.

**Many sources were blocked.** This environment's network policy blocked arXiv, Hugging Face, sqlite.org, machinelearning.apple.com, support.apple.com, apple.com and many vendor sites, including Chroma, NVIDIA, Jina, Vectara, Qdrant, AWS, Microsoft Learn, ACL Anthology and OpenReview. Several figures therefore rest on search summaries and are tagged that way. They include AFM 3's parameter counts, MiniLM's 128-token training length, Chroma's chunking results, SQuAD 2.0, MiniCheck and Elastic's fusion sample size.

**Apple availability comes from documentation metadata.** It was read from DocC platform metadata, not from the SDK's `@available` lines. The `ContextOptions` correction and the removal of adapters are therefore not confirmed against the iOS 27 interface. The 8,192 figure appears only in WWDC26 code comments, and no Apple page maps a variant to a window size.

**No measurement covers the owner's documents.** Almost every retrieval-quality number in the repository comes from QASPER research papers, at settings that differ from the shipped build. None covers personal documents, and none measures the precision path's false-lock rate. There is no on-device latency or memory figure for any proposed checker, embedder or reranker.

**Some inputs were taken as given.** Roadmap rows were read by title, and in the storage audit by page content, but they were not re-scoped; the statuses here describe code, not the rows' acceptance criteria. The incident's facts come from the owner's report, not from re-read logs.

## Conclusion

The trust gradient in the app runs backwards. AFM 3 is treated as an untrusted text generator whose output five heuristic sites may overwrite, yet the heuristics themselves are unmeasured, and they are what produced this failure [code_verified, exact for the five sites; inferred, high for the rest]. The same inversion shows in retrieval. The trained cross-encoder adds the most, and the stages after it give back more than a quarter of its rank-1 wins before the prompt is built [artifact_derived, high; `LEDGER.md:1106-1160`]. The fundamental work therefore removes more code than it adds: stop the stale re-sort and the per-batch normalisation, let extraction propose evidence rather than answers, and make a label mean that a check ran.

The binding constraint is proof, not architecture or model choice. Apple's 2026 toolkit supplies nearly every missing primitive: Evaluations, `tokenCount`, `contextSize`, `variant`, and evidence-first guided generation. It also closes one door, fine-tuning adapters [documented, fetched 2026-09-26], so the improvement has to come from the pipeline rather than the model. Nothing here, the ten lines included, can be declared fixed by re-asking one question on one device. A Standard re-ask can come back from a cache that nothing clears [confirmed twice], and nearly all the evidence the repository holds is about research papers, not leases [artifact_derived, high].
