Part one. The retrieval formulas as implemented.

Cosine similarity. Cos of q and d equals q dot d over the product of their L two norms. Vectors are unit-normalised at write time, so at query time this reduces to q dot d, computed by V D S P dot product per vector or V D S P matrix multiply over the mapped buffer, or by the Metal batch cosine kernel above one thousand documents. Norms are still stored, in the norms file, for the C P U matrix path, which computes raw dot products and normalises after.

B M twenty-five. Score of q and d equals the sum over query terms of I D F of t, times f of t in d, times k one plus one, over f of t in d plus k one times one minus b plus b times document length over average document length. The F T S five implementation is invoked with per-column weights: ten point zero for section title, five point zero for section path, one point zero for content, and zero for every unindexed column. The fallback in-process implementation uses k one of approximately one point five and b of approximately zero point five, with I D F computed as log of total documents minus document frequency plus zero point five over document frequency plus zero point five, plus one.

Reciprocal rank fusion. R R F of d equals the sum over result lists r of one over k plus rank of d in r, with k equal to sixty exactly, and the two lists weighted zero point seven for vector and zero point three for keyword before summation. Ties are broken deterministically by chunk identifier, because Swift's sort is not stable and equal scores are routine.

Maximal marginal relevance. M M R of d equals lambda times relevance of d to q, minus one minus lambda times the maximum similarity between d and any already selected document. Lambda is zero point six zero, zero point five five, zero point five zero by mode. The pairwise matrix is computed on Metal when candidates exceed fifty and the profile permits Metal vector operations.

Evidence capacity. Available evidence tokens equals model context minus instructions minus tool schema overhead minus question minus conversation minus safety reserve minus reserved output. Base context four thousand and ninety-six, default budget three thousand two hundred, safety reserve two hundred and fifty-six, character-to-token ratio one point four on device and two point five for the cloud estimate. Part three. The reranker.

A cross-encoder Core M L model with its own Rust-backed tokenizer. Pairs are encoded to a maximum length of five hundred and twelve. Prediction is called per pair inside a task group, on the basis that the Core M L prediction call is thread-safe with internal synchronisation. Compute units all, low-precision accumulation on G P U allowed. Loaded once at engine initialisation in a detached task; if the load fails, rerank is skipped and heuristic scoring applies. The model file is the re-ranker model package in the app's M L models directory. The architecture name is not stated in code; the word bank calls it Tiny BERT and that name was not verified here.
